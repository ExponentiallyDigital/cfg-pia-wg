Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 31 July, 2026

Exponentially Digital ("we", "our", or "us") operates the cfg-pia-wg mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") outlines our strict data handling practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

1. Information collection and use

Our core privacy philosophy is simple: we do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal information to our own servers. Credentials entered in the app are transmitted exclusively to Private Internet Access (PIA) as described below, and never to us.

A. Account credentials

To generate WireGuard configurations, the app requires your PIA username and password.

Transmission: your credentials are transmitted using HTTP Basic Authentication over a direct HTTPS-encrypted connection exclusively to PIA's official authentication endpoint (https://www.privateinternetaccess.com/gtoken/generateToken). No third party ever receives your credentials. The sole purpose of this call is to obtain a short-lived PIA session token. Once that token is received, your username and password play no further part in any operation.

Storage: your credentials are never written to your device's persistent storage. They are held only in Flutter text field controllers in application memory for the duration of your active session.

Session clearing: your credentials are cleared from application memory on exiting the app.

Password field protections: the password field is obscured by default. As you type, each character is briefly visible before being replaced with a dot, which is standard behaviour provided by the Flutter framework. The copy action is disabled on the password field, consistent with Flutter's default handling of obscured text inputs. Autocorrect and keyboard input suggestions are disabled on both the username and password fields, which prevents keyboard applications from recording or caching your credentials in their own suggestion engines or dictionaries.

Screen security:  when the app is placed in the background or the task switcher is opened, the app's screen contents are not displayed.

B. Generated configurations

The WireGuard configuration profiles (`.conf` files) generated by this App are built dynamically in your device's volatile memory. They are never collected or seen by us. When utilising the system sharing utilities, files are written to temporary system directories and immediately wiped upon completion of the operation.

C. Watchdog WireGuard management function

If you use this function, your PIA username and password are stored in your router's non volatile memory, this is required for the self-healing shell script to automatically reconfigure a VPN interface.

2. Third-party service providers

The App communicates directly with PIA APIs to fetch server regions and establish configuration profiles. While our App does not gather your data, your interactions with PIA's servers are subject to the privacy terms and conditions managed independently by PIA.

During WireGuard key registration, the App also downloads and caches the PIA CA certificate from the official PIA open-source repository hosted on GitHub (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pia-foss/manual-connections/master/ca.rsa.4096.crt). No personal data is included in or derived from this request. This connection is made solely to verify the authenticity of the PIA server. Your interactions with GitHub's servers are subject to the privacy terms and conditions managed independently by GitHub.

Hotlinks to this Privacy Policy, the Security Policy, application documentation, and source code are available in the app. All content is hosted on GitHub, except for this Policy document which is hosted on www.exponentiallydigital.com.

3. Data safety & security

The App's runtime operates entirely within Android's application security sandbox. The WireGuard keypair generated during each session uses the cryptographically secure random number generation (SecureRandom) provided by the Android platform.

The complete source code for this App is publicly available on our GitHub repository, allowing independent verification of the data handling practices described in this policy. Build instructions are included for users who wish to compile and install their own copy.

4. Children's privacy

Our App does not collect any data and does not target or knowingly solicit information from anyone under the age of 13.

5. Changes to this privacy policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any modifications will be reflected directly on this page with an updated effective date.
